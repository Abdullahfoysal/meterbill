FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does the electricity bill tracker work offline?

Yes. Offline mode is supported for up to 5 days, and you can calculate and print bills without an internet connection.

Can I track more than one electricity meter?

Yes. Add unlimited meters, organise them under a location or area name, and track each one separately with full bill history.

Does the app process payments?

No. It is a utility record-management tool for tracking paid and due bills. It does not process card payments or digital purchases inside the app.

Is my data safe?

Sign in with Google and your records are stored securely with Firebase, synced in real time across your devices.

Is the app free?

The app is free to download on Google Play. Some features or service access may require administrator approval or an active service plan arranged outside the app.
